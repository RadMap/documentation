Third-party software

RadMap includes software and data made by others. The copyright notice and the full licence text of every component RadMap or its installer ships are in ThirdPartyNotices.txt, in the Doc folder of the installation directory, and in Help → About. This section gives the notices whose licences ask for them in the user documentation.

Geant4

DoseAlgorithm, which runs RadMap’s simulations, uses the Geant4 toolkit for the radiation transport.

This product includes software developed by Members of the Geant4 Collaboration ( http://cern.ch/geant4 ).

RadMap ships a modified version of Geant4, built from RadMap’s fork of it, https://github.com/RadMap/geant4; it is not the Geant4 Collaboration’s release, and RadMap is not endorsed by the Geant4 Collaboration.

The Geant4 data sets RadMap installs (G4EMLOW, G4ENSDFSTATE, PhotonEvaporation and RadioactiveDecay) are distributed by the Geant4 Collaboration with the toolkit. They are derived from evaluated nuclear and atomic data, among them ENSDF and the Livermore libraries (EPDL97, EEDL, EADL, EPICS2014).
